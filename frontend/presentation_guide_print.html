5. Guide Q&A Cheat-Sheet (Bulletproof Answers)

Q: "Why did you build 4 stakeholder lenses over the same data instead of separate apps?"
Answer: "In enterprise banking, separate tools create fragmented data silos and conflicting metrics between Risk and Business. Under our decoupled gateway architecture, all 4 stakeholders view the exact same live case (NCB-CASE-7891), audit log, and cost telemetry, but through a lens customized to their regulatory responsibility."
Q: "What is the key takeaway between a one-way and two-way door?"
Answer: "As taught in Slide 2 and 5, a two-way door is reversible with low cost (e.g., routing traffic to Gemini backup when Azure hits a 40% quota cap). A one-way door is irreversible (e.g., removing human sign-off creates immediate regulatory liability under SMCR). One-way doors are strictly flagged and cannot be overridden by engineers."
Q: "How does this prototype satisfy delivery leadership without coding today?"
Answer: "Slide 4 emphasizes '0 lines of code today' because real delivery leadership is about orchestration: aligning 7 functions, managing dependencies, maintaining an immutable decision log, and escalating early with options rather than just problems."
