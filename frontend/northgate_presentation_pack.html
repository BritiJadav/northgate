  • • Document Scope: Registry extracts (Companies House, KVK, ACRA) + Articles + Passports.

  • • Singapore Complex Trusts: Excludes cross-border multi-tier Asian trusts (>5 tiers).
  • • Autonomous Decisions: System NEVER assigns risk rating; human officer signs.
  • • Client Messaging: System NEVER emails clients directly; RM/analyst reviews & sends.
  • • Core Banking Edits: Zero changes to mainframe overnight batch processing.
ADR-001 · First slice writes drafts only; a person signs every decision Two-Way Door

Decision: A new service gathers filings, extracts the ownership chain, and builds one complete gap list through the bank's AI Gateway. Its output is saved as unsealed DRAFTS against the case in the KYC platform. An analyst confirms every fact and sends the client one request. A person assigns the risk rating. No new data store; no change to core banking.

Revisit If: Model Risk classifies drafts as influencing a risk outcome; or analysts reject > 1 in 5 drafted chains; or screening/registry costs exceed monthly budget.
